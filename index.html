The Method

In the sigil magic of Austin Osman Spare, later popularised by Grant Morrison, a desire is written out, its vowels and repeated letters are struck away, and the remaining letters are woven into a glyph. The glyph is then charged, by gazing at it in a state of absorbed attention, and then forgotten, so that the conscious mind stops worrying at it.

This engine performs that rite in sound. Your reduced consonants are hashed with SHA-256. The hash arranges the twenty-one consonants around a wheel, and your letters trace a path across it. That path is then read as a score.

How the line becomes music

Angle on the wheelScale degree. The wheel holds three octaves of seven positions, so the faint triangle marks the tonic.

Length of a strokeHow long the note is held

Rotating the sigilTransposing the melody
Mirroring the sigilInverting the melody
Tracing it backwardsPlaying the melody in retrograde
Polygons at the centreEuclidean rhythms: beats spaced as evenly as possible around a circle

The rite

Charge opens a fullscreen mode with a breathing guide that is synchronised to the music. Forget burns the sigil, clears your words, and returns you to silence. Nothing you type ever leaves this page. Share sends only the consonants, which is the sigil itself and not the desire behind it.
